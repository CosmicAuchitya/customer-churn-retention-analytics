1. Executive Mindset: Subscription vs. E-Commerce Model

Interviews mein sabse pehla farq yahi poocha jata hai:

  • Contractual Subscription (Telco/Netflix): Explicit cancellation date hoti hai (Churn = 1). Customer jab cancel karta hai, company ko turant pata chal jata hai. Focus hota hai: Monthly Churn Rate (%) aur Lost MRR/ARR ($) par.
  • Transactional E-Commerce (Amazon/Flipkart): Koi contract ya expiry date nahi hoti. Customer bina bataye khareedna band kar deta hai (Latent Churn). Focus hota hai: Cohort Retention Triangle (decay curve) aur RFM Segmentation (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) par.

2. Module 1: Data Hygiene & TotalCharges Bug

Pehle Business Question Kya Tha? Data load karte hi TotalCharges column float hone ke bajaye Object (Text) kyu tha? 
# Empty string ko NaN banakar 0.0 se fill karna
blank_charges = df[df['TotalCharges'].str.strip() == '']
df['TotalCharges'] = pd.to_numeric(df['TotalCharges'], errors='coerce').fillna(0.0)
df['Churn_Numeric'] = (df['Churn'] == 'Yes').astype(int)

Reasoning: Dataset mein 11 records aise the jahan customer ne kal hi join kiya tha (tenure == 0) aur unka pehla bill generate nahi hua tha. Database ne space (" ") daal diya tha. Humne pd.to_numeric(errors='coerce') karke fillna(0.0) kiya taaki calculations mathematically accurate rahein.

🗣️ Interview Defense Script:
"During initial data profiling, I identified 11 zero-tenure records where TotalCharges contained whitespace strings. Rather than dropping them or using mean imputation, I coerced them to float and imputed zero charges, preserving newly onboarded accounts."
