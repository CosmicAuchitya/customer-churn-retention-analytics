## 🔬 Core Forensic Investigations (Subscription Model)

### 1. The Month-to-Month Contract Death Trap
- **The Finding:** Month-to-month contracts represent 55.0% of all accounts (3,875 customers), but drive **88.55% of all churn events (1,655 churned accounts)**.
- **Churn Rate:**
  - **Month-to-month:** **42.71%** ($1,450,165 ARR lost).
  - **One year:** **11.27%** ($169,421 ARR lost).
  - **Two year:** **2.83%** ($49,983 ARR lost).
- **Executive Diagnosis:** A customer on a 2-year contract is **15x less likely to churn** than a month-to-month subscriber.

---

### 2. The Fiber Optic Paradox & "The Tech Support Moat"
- **The Finding:** The company's highest-priced product (**Fiber Optic**, $91.50/mo average bill) suffers from the worst churn rate across all categories (**41.89%** vs DSL at 18.96% and No Internet at 7.40%).
- **The Service Bundling Discovery:**
  - **Fiber Optic with Zero Protection (No Tech Support, No Security):** **55.01% Churn Rate** ($86.10 AOV).
  - **Fiber Optic with Partial Protection (One Add-On):** **28.53% Churn Rate** ($95.90 AOV).
  - **Fiber Optic with Full Security Bundle (Both Add-Ons Active):** **14.17% Churn Rate** ($105.73 AOV).
- **Core Takeaway:** Bundling Tech Support and Online Security slashes Fiber Optic churn by **40.8 percentage points**, while increasing monthly ARPU from $86 to $105! Customers churn due to lack of support during technical glitches, not high pricing.

---

### 3. Payment Method Friction & The "Pain of Paying"
- **The Finding:**
  - **Electronic Check (Manual Payment):** **45.29% Churn Rate** (Accounts for **57.30% of all churn**, bleeding $1.01M in annual ARR!).
  - **Mailed Check:** **19.11% Churn Rate**.
  - **Bank Transfer (Automatic):** **16.71% Churn Rate**.
  - **Credit Card (Automatic):** **15.24% Churn Rate**.
- **Psychological Root Cause:** Automated payments remove monthly cognitive friction. Manual electronic checks trigger recurring "Pain of Paying" evaluation events every billing cycle.

---

### 4. Tenure Hazard & The Critical 12-Month Window
- **0–12 Months (Onboarding Cohort):** **47.44% Churn Rate** (1,037 accounts lost, driving **55.48% of all churn**).
- **13–24 Months:** **28.71% Churn Rate**.
- **25–48 Months:** **19.39% Churn Rate**.
- **49–72 Months (Loyal Champions):** **9.51% Churn Rate**.
- **Strategy:** Retention budgets must be front-loaded into the first 90–180 days of onboarding.
